# Abigail tribute gallery provenance

Internal note for the `/abigail` tribute page. Not public page copy.

Eleven photographs were selected and visually audited for dignity and variety: one hero portrait plus ten supporting gallery images. All were converted to self-hosted WebP under `public/images/abigail/` with EXIF orientation applied, natural aspect ratios preserved and the longest edge capped at 2200px (hero) or 2000px (gallery).

## Publication status

- The ten images sourced from the OAKonsult-controlled SharePoint (the Adeloye family OneDrive folder) are for **staging review only**. They require story-owner/family publication confirmation before any production promotion.
- `abigail-award-moment.webp` comes from the already publicly published OAKonsult WordPress media library and needs no further confirmation.
- No private source URLs, item identifiers or access credentials are recorded here by design.
