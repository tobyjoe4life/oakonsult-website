## Abigail facts used publicly

- Full name: Oluwatoyitan Abigail Chikatara Adeloye
- Born: 25 September 2008
- Died: 3 October 2024
- The life-changing medical incident occurred at 18 months in April 2010
- She loved music, bright colours, cuddles, laughter, her sisters and family prayer
